The illustrations were rendered from the project KiCad/STEP models, the official Raspberry Pi 5 model, and simplified accessory models. Exploded distances and pulled-back plugs show assembly relationships; they are not measurements for manufacturing. Cable housing shapes may differ from the parts supplied.

The Raspberry Pi 5 model is from [Raspberry Pi's product information portal](https://pip.raspberrypi.com/categories/892-raspberry-pi-5). Its [license and original notice](Raspberry-Pi-model-LICENSE.txt) are retained here. The active cooler follows [Raspberry Pi's mechanical drawing](https://pip-assets.raspberrypi.com/categories/993-raspberry-pi-active-cooler/documents/RP-008187-DS-1-raspberry-pi-active-cooler-mechanical-drawing.pdf). The I-PAC2 and PACLED64 illustrations use mounting drawings supplied by Ultimarc; they are simplified representations, not official complete product models.
