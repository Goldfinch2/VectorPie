Ordering

How do reservations work?
BeamForge is built in production runs. Reserve on the reservation page with your email: it's free and commits you to nothing. Once enough boards are reserved, we email everyone on the list a link to order. You pay only then.
Can I change or cancel a reservation?
Yes. Email us and we remove you from the list. Nothing to refund, since you haven't paid.
Can I cancel?
Yes. Cancel any time before your board ships and you get a full refund to your PayPal account or card. Email us with your PayPal transaction ID.
What if it is sold out?
Boards are made in limited batches. When a batch is gone, the button shows sold out until the next one.
How do I pay?
With PayPal only: your PayPal balance, or a credit or debit card through PayPal's checkout (no PayPal account needed in most countries). Prices are in USD. We never see your card details.

My order

There are no accounts on this site. After you pay, PayPal emails you a receipt with a transaction ID: that is your order confirmation. We email you again when your board ships, with the tracking number. To check on an order, email us with the transaction ID.

Shipping

Orders ship from Canada with Canada Post to Canada, the United States (delivered by USPS) and Europe. Shipping is one flat rate of 50 USD per order, shown at checkout and in the shipping policy. Duties and import taxes, including European import VAT, are paid by the buyer on delivery.

Refunds

Full refund on any order cancelled before it ships. For a board that has shipped, see the refund policy.
