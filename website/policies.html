Shipping policy

We ship from with Canada Post. We ship to Canada, the United States and Europe only; PayPal's checkout accepts addresses in those countries only.

  • Shipping is one flat rate of 50 USD per order, to every region, charged at checkout. All prices are in USD.
  • In the United States the parcel is delivered by USPS; in Europe by the local postal service.
  • Every parcel has a tracking number, sent to you by email when it ships.

Duties and import taxes

Duties, import taxes and carrier brokerage or handling fees are not included in the price or the shipping rate. They are paid by the buyer on delivery.

  • Europe: your country charges import VAT on the value of the order, and the postal service may add a handling fee.
  • United States: US import rules for small parcels changed recently: parcels that used to enter duty-free may now owe duty or fees on delivery, depending on the product's country of origin. The customs form on every parcel states the country of origin.
  • Canada: shipped domestically, no import charges.

A parcel refused at delivery because of import charges is returned to us; we refund the order minus the shipping cost both ways.

Refund & return policy

Before shipping

  • Cancel any time before your order ships and you get a full refund, including shipping.
  • Refunds go back through PayPal to the account or card you paid with.

After shipping

  • Arrived damaged or doesn't work: email us within 30 days of delivery with your transaction ID and a description or photo. We repair, replace or refund it, and pay the return shipping.
  • Changed your mind: return an unused board in its original packaging within 30 days of delivery for a refund of the item price. Return shipping is paid by the buyer.

A vector monitor works with high voltages. Follow the installation guide, and turn the cabinet off and unplug it before installing the board.

Privacy policy

  • Orders: PayPal sends us your name, shipping address, email address and what you bought. We use them only to ship your order and to contact you about it. We never see your card details.
  • Sharing: we share your address with the carrier, and nothing with anyone else. We don't sell personal data.
  • This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics. PayPal's checkout and button, and the email signup service if used, follow their own privacy policies.
  • Ask us at any time to see or delete the data we hold about you.
